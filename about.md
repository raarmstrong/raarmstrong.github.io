I am a clinical academic based in Bristol, UK, working at the intersection of anaesthesia, genetics, and data science.

Currently, I am:
* **PhD Fellow** on the GW4-CAT PhD Programme for Health Professionals, studying the use of multiomics in predicting and understanding immune-mediated complications of anaesthesia and surgery.
* **Anaesthetist** in the Severn Deanery, NHS.
* **Research Fellow** at the Royal College of Anaesthetists Centre for Research and Improvement (CR&I).

---

## Research Focus

My work aims to bridge the gap between molecular profiling and bedside clinical decision-making. I focus on:
* **Biobank-Scale Genomics:** Utilizing datasets like the UK Biobank to analyze the genomic architecture of postoperative delirium and surgical complications.
* **Clinical Prediction Models:** Building and validating machine learning pipelines to identify high-risk patients.
* **Causal Inference:** Applying causal machine learning frameworks to clinical data to discover actionable targets for improving surgical outcomes.
