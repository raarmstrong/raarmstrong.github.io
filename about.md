I am a clinical academic working at the intersection of anaesthesia, multiomics, and data science.

Currently, I am:
* **PhD Fellow** on the GW4-CAT PhD for Health Professionals Programme, studying the use of multiomics in predicting and understanding immune-mediated complications of anaesthesia and surgery.
* **Anaesthetist** in the Severn Deanery, NHS.

---

## Research Focus

My work aims to bridge the gap between molecular profiling and bedside clinical decision-making. I focus on:
* **Biobank-Scale Multiomics:** Utilizing datasets like the UK Biobank to analyse the multiomic architecture of postoperative complications.
* **Clinical Prediction Models:** Building and validating machine learning pipelines to identify high-risk patients.
* **Causal Inference:** Applying causal machine learning frameworks to clinical data to discover actionable targets for improving surgical outcomes.
