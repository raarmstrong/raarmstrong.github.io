I am an anaesthetist in the Severn Deanery and a GW4-CAT PhD Fellow studying the use of multiomics in predicting and understanding immune-mediated complications of anaesthesia and surgery. My research interests focus on applying machine learning and causal inference to improve patient outcomes after major surgery.

GitHub LinkedIn Twitter iD ORCID RG ResearchGate

Research Pillars

Multiomics & Biobank Data

Analyzing large-scale genetic and multiomic datasets (like the UK Biobank) to identify molecular pathways and predict surgical complications.

Clinical Machine Learning

Developing and deploying robust adaptive predictive pipelines to identify patients at risk of post-operative complications and delirium.

Causal Inference

Applying causal machine learning methods to routinely collected health data and omics data to discover targetable drivers of clinical outcomes.
